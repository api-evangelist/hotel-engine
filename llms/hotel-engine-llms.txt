# Engine: All-in-one business travel and spend platform for lodging, flights, rental cars, group travel, meeting and event spaces, travel policy, approvals, and the Engine X charge card

> Engine is the all-in-one business travel and spend platform for booking and managing lodging, flights, rental cars, group travel, meeting and event spaces, travel policy, approvals, and payments with the Engine X charge card. Headquartered in Denver, Colorado and led by CEO Elia Wallen, Engine is trusted by 25,000+ businesses and 1.2 million travelers, with negotiated rates across 1,000,000+ hotel properties worldwide. Engine charges no membership fee, no contracts, no minimum spend, and no agent-assist fees.

Engine rebranded from "Hotel Engine" to "Engine" in Summer 2024 to reflect its expansion beyond lodging into a full business travel and spend platform. Companies use Engine to book and manage business lodging, flights, rental cars, group travel, and meeting and event spaces, set and enforce travel policy, route trip approvals, pay for travel through the Engine X charge card, and provide duty of care to traveling employees through Engine's partnership with International SOS.

Engine serves businesses of every size, from sole proprietors and small teams to large enterprises (including a number of Fortune 100 companies), across most industries. For canonical, AI-ready company facts, start with the AI Information page below.

## Products

- [Engine Groups](https://www.engine.com/groups): Group travel for 9 or more rooms, including work crews, construction projects, corporate events, sports teams, and weddings.
- [Engine Spaces](https://www.engine.com/spaces): Meeting and event space booking for offsites, conferences, training sessions, and board meetings.
- [Engine X](https://www.engine.com/x): Engine's charge card built for business travel and everyday business spend, paying tiered rewards on hotels and rental cars booked through Engine and 1.5% on all other business spend. Issued by Fifth Third Bank, N.A., Member FDIC, on the Visa Commercial network.

## Platform capabilities

- [Travel policy](https://www.engine.com/corporate-travel-policies): Set and enforce travel policy with three distinct enforcement modes (Block, Allowed with Reason, and Approval), a level of control most booking tools do not offer.
